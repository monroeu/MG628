Meet Your Professor

Welcome to the course

Hello everyone

My name is Professor Fomba Kassoh, and I will be your instructor for this course.

My academic and professional background spans Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Business Analytics, Civil Engineering, Transportation, and Water Resources. In addition to teaching at Monroe University, I work in the transportation industry, where I manage technology and operational projects involving data, automated systems, and decision-making.

How we will learn

My goal in this class is to help you go beyond simply learning concepts. I want you to understand how to apply data, analytical tools, and technology to solve real-world problems.

We will focus on practical skills that you can use in your academic work, your career, and everyday decision-making. I look forward to working with you and to a productive semester together.

Course Overview

What MG628 is about

The course develops quantitative and analytical decision-making skills through Excel, Python, SQL, data visualization, statistics, probability, modeling, and business intelligence. Students work with real-world business and healthcare contexts and complete a culminating project.

Credits3
PrerequisiteKG 578 Business Analytics Foundations

Learning Objectives

What you should be able to do

  • Demonstrate basic proficiency in Python for data manipulation and analysis.
  • Use data analysis tools and techniques to support business and organizational decisions.
  • Analyze data, identify patterns, and make informed decisions in different contexts.
  • Use critical thinking to evaluate information and communicate findings effectively.
  • Apply statistics, probability, and mathematical models to real-world business problems.
  • Gather, clean, analyze, and visualize data using Excel, SQL, Power BI, Tableau, and related tools.
  • Extract knowledge and insights from large datasets and communicate patterns, trends, and outliers.
