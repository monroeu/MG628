What you need

Prepare

A computer with Power BI Desktop and/or Tableau Desktop/Tableau Public authoring access. Download the dataset files from the lab pages.

Practice

Follow each guided section, confirm the expected row counts and computed KPIs, and save your workbook locally.

Submit

Export workbook and evidence screenshots as required by your instructor. With course login and an instructor-published offering, lab progress and submissions are saved in Supabase.

Lab principles

Both manuals distinguish illustrative benchmark figures from computed source-data results; warn when missing calendar dates can distort charts; and require students to interpret results rather than merely produce screenshots.
